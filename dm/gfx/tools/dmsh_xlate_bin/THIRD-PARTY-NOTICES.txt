Third-party notices for dmsh_xlate

dmsh_xlate is a native executable. It is built from dm/gfx/tools/dmsh_xlate by
dm/tools/native_tools.sh with .NET NativeAOT, so it contains compiled code from
the projects below. Each keeps its own license.

1. Ryujinx's shader translator: Ryujinx.Graphics.Shader, Ryujinx.Common and
   Spv.Generator from Ryujinx 1.3.2, with NX 18's changes. MIT.
   The source and its licenses are in this repository:
   - dm/third_party/ryujinx/LICENSE.txt (Ryujinx Team and Contributors)
   - dm/third_party/ryujinx/src/Spv.Generator/LICENSE (Spv.Generator's own
     license text)
   dm/third_party/ryujinx/README.md lists the changes. Both texts are also
   copied below.

2. The .NET runtime and libraries (.NET 10), by the .NET Foundation and
   Contributors. MIT.
   https://github.com/dotnet/runtime
   The runtime includes code from other projects, among them zlib-ng, which is
   linked into this executable. Their notices are in the runtime's
   THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.TXT:
   https://github.com/dotnet/runtime/blob/main/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.TXT

3. MacNativeGames, by the MacNativeGames contributors. MIT.
   https://github.com/ly05010419/MacNativeGames
   Two pieces are compiled in: the visibility-counter lowering in Program.cs
   (VertexCounterLowering), and the side-effect check of RemoveNode in
   Ryujinx's Optimizer.cs, which follows its translator patch 0002. That patch
   comes from commit 01a1e37f (by ssskkk12345) of sunsssyc's Ryujinx_Optime
   (MIT, https://github.com/sunsssyc/Ryujinx_Optime).
   The license is dm/third_party/macnativegames/LICENSE, copied below.
